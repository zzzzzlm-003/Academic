More Examples
Economic Networks
• Trade Networks: Connections between countries, cities, or entities based
  on trade relationships and routes.
• Financial Networks: Banks, institutions, and individuals interconnected
  through loans, deposits, and other financial instruments.
Biological Networks
• Neural Networks: Interconnected neurons in a brain or nervous system.
• Metabolic Networks: Enzymes and metabolites in a biological system,
  representing how various biochemical processes interact.
• Ecological Networks: Predators and prey, symbiotic relationships, and
  other interactions among species in an ecosystem.

What is NetworkX?

● NetworkX is a Python package to create, manipulate, and study
  complex networks
● Useful for modeling optimization problems: shortest path, flows,
  connectivity
● Works with both undirected and directed (graph and digraph)
  networks
● Integrates well with NumPy, matplotlib for analyses and
  visualization



                                                                     26
What Can NetworkX Do?

● Build graphs (nodes & edges) programmatically
● Visualize networks with labels and custom colors
● Analyze properties: shortest paths, connected components,
  cycles
● Model real-world optimization problems (transport, flows,
  logistics)
● Combine with solvers or OR libraries (like Gurobi, PuLP, SciPy)

Example: Project Management Problem


Background:

A company wants to introduce a new model of its ever-popular cell
phone to the market. To gauge the demand, a limited batch of product
is to be brought to the market. The product is assembled from two
parts, a circuit board, and housing. In addition, workers need to be
trained and raw material procured.




                                                                       61
Example: Project Management Problem

After the initial assessment, the project manager put together a list of
all tasks along with estimated duration of each task. The tasks are
interdependent, some need to finish before others can start.


First, (A) workers need to be trained and (B) raw materials purchased
which takes 6 and 9 days, respectively. After these tasks are finished,
both parts of the product, (C) the circuit board and (D) the housing,
are manufactured taking 7 and 8 days, respectively. Each circuit
board, once manufactured, needs to undergo additional testing (E) to
comply with FCC regulation which takes 10 days. Afterwards, the cell
phone is assembled, packaged, and shipped (F) which takes 12 days.

Example: Project Management Problem

What is the minimum number of days before the product can reach the
market? What is the critical path of the production, i.e., the sequence of
tasks delaying any of which delays the whole production?
